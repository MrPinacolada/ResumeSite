# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Tech recruiters, engineering managers, and potential clients evaluating Vasilii Skovorodin (NinjaDev) as a senior frontend developer / team lead. They land from LinkedIn, a CV link, or a referral, usually skim for 30-60 seconds, often on mobile, and decide whether to reach out. They want fast proof of craft, real shipped work, and a sense of the person.

## Product Purpose
A single-page personal portfolio at devninja.net that turns a skim into a contact. It has to make the craft self-evident (the site itself is the work sample), surface the strongest projects with links, and make the contact step obvious. Success = the visitor leaves with a clear impression and reaches out (Telegram / email / LinkedIn) or downloads the CV.

## Brand Personality
Confident, sharp, playful. A "ninja" frontend dev: precise and fast, but with a sense of humor (a dino-runner in the header, a playable tank game, a particle web). Voice is direct and first-person, not corporate. Three words: bold, precise, mischievous.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS-purple-gradient-on-white dev portfolios (the current look, and the default AI aesthetic).
- Soft, timid, evenly-distributed pastel palettes; floating identical rounded cards with drop shadows.
- The saturated editorial-typographic lane: display-serif italics + tiny mono eyebrows + ruled separators + monochrome restraint. We want brutalist, not magazine.
- Corporate "we streamline / empower / supercharge" copy.

## Design Principles
1. **The site is the proof.** Craft, performance, and motion quality are the actual portfolio piece, not just the projects shown.
2. **Loud structure, quiet noise.** Big confident type and hard color blocks carry impact; decoration (particles, games) stays in the background and never fights the content.
3. **Keep the mischief.** The dino runner, tanks, and particle web are part of who he is. Polish them, don't sand them off.
4. **Honest hierarchy.** One thing matters per screen: name, then work, then contact. Recruiters skim, so the path is obvious.
5. **Fast and accessible.** Real text (not images of text), readable contrast, reduced-motion support, works on a phone in one hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text >=4.5:1, large text >=3:1, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and headings. Every scroll/hover animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (instant or crossfade). The playful games are enhancements, never the only path to information.
